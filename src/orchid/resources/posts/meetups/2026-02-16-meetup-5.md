# 🎉 Hey curious kotliners! 

How long has it been? A year? An eternity? 🤔 We're not sure, we lost count somewhere between val and var! But the long, dark, Kotlin-less night is over! ☀️

That's right, the Kotlin Meetup is BACK, and we're kicking things off with a new fantastic host. Dust off your social skills and get ready to dive back into the world of null safety and extension functions! ✨

We're incredibly excited to be hosted by the security wizards at Nord Security 🛡️ in their awesome Cyber City office. They're providing the space, the snacks, and the good vibes—all you need to bring is your love for Kotlin. ❤️

Join us for an evening of learning, networking, and celebrating the fact that we finally remembered the password to the meetup page. 🥳

📅 __Save the Date:__ 2026-03-05 </br>
🕒 __Event Time:__ 18:00 - 20:00 </br>
📍 __Location:__ Cyber City, Švitrigailos g. 36

### 🚀 Time Slots:

__18:00__ Doors opens</br>
__18:30 - 19:00 Justas Medeišis__ "Agents for Kotlin, Kotlin for agents"</br>
Brief common knowledge based on practical experiments with Koog. An overview of Jetbrains framework to build AI agents.</br>
__19:00 - 19:30__ Break. There'll be snacks—because even robots need fuel!</br>
__19:30-20:00 Robertas Šetkus__ - "Curious Cases of Kotlin FP"</br>
Drawing from the practical wisdom in Functional Programming, ideas with a higher impact on Kotlin code, including how to model data in immutable fashion, describing dependencies using contexts and effects, or treating actions as data.
